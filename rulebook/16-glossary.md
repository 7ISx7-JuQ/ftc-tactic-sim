# 16 Glossary

The following definitions and terms are used for the _FIRST_ Tech Challenge game BIOBUZZ. Defined terms are in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS throughout the manual (for example, ALLIANCE). Competition rules mean what they plainly say. If a word is not given a game definition, then you should use its common conversational meaning.

|**Term**|**Definition**|
|---|---|
|**ALLIANCE**|a cooperative of 2 _FIRST_ Tech Challenge teams|
|**ALLIANCE AREA**|an approximately 97 in. (246.40 cm) wide by 54 in. (137.15 cm) deep by<br>infinitely tall volume formed by placing ALLIANCE colored tape onto the<br>flooring surface outside of the FIELD|
|**ALLIANCE CAPTAIN**|the designated STUDENT representative from each ALLIANCE lead|
|**ARENA**|includes all elements of the game infrastructure that are required to play this<br>season's FTC game including: the FIELD, SCORING ELEMENTS, queue area,<br>team media area, and all equipment needed for FIELD control, ROBOT<br>control, and scorekeeping|
|**ARENA FAULT**|an error in ARENA operation|
|**AUTO**|the first 30 seconds of the MATCH, during which DRIVERS may not provide<br>input to their ROBOTS, so ROBOTS operate with only their pre-programmed<br>instructions|
|**CELL**|a three-dimensional structure that can hold NECTAR and POLLEN|
|**CHASSIS**|ROBOT’S MAJOR MECHANISM that enables it to move around a FIELD|
|**COMPONENT**|any part in its most basic configuration, which cannot be disassembled<br>without damaging or destroying the part or altering its fundamental function|
|**CONTINUOUS**|describes durations that are more than approximately 10 seconds|
|**CONTROL**|an action by a ROBOT in which the SCORING ELEMENT is fully supported by<br>or stuck in, on, or under the ROBOT or it intentionally pushes a SCORING<br>ELEMENT to a desired location or in a preferred direction (i.e., herding).<br>CONTROL requires contact with a ROBOT, either directly or transitively<br>through other SCORING ELEMENTS. Typically, CONTROL requires one of the<br>following to be true:<br>A. The SCORING ELEMENT is fully supported by the ROBOT<br>B. The ROBOT is moving the SCORING ELEMENT in a preferred direction<br>with a flat or concave face of the ROBOT|
|**COTS**|a standard (i.e., not custom order) part commonly available from a VENDOR<br>for all teams for purchase|
|**CUSTOM CIRCUIT**|any active electrical item that is not an actuator (specified in R501) or power<br>regulation device (specified in R505)|
|**DISABLED**|The REFEREE instructs the team to stop the ROBOT which will deactivate all<br>outputs, rendering the ROBOT inoperable for the remainder of the MATCH|
|**DISQUALIFIED**|the state of a team in which they receive 0 MATCH points and 0 RANKING<br>POINTS in a Qualification MATCH or causes their ALLIANCE to receive 0<br>MATCH points in a Playoff MATCH|
|**DRIVE COACH**|a guide or advisor|
|**DRIVE TEAM**|a set of up to 4 people from the same _FIRST_ Tech Challenge team<br>responsible for team performance for a specific MATCH|
|**DRIVER**|an operator and controller of the ROBOT|
|**DRIVER STATION**|Android device (smartphone or REV Driver Hub) that runs the DRIVER<br>STATION App software to communicate with a ROBOT per R901|
|**FABRICATED ITEM**|any COMPONENT or MECHANISM that has been altered, built, cast,<br>constructed, concocted, created, cut, heat treated, machined, manufactured,<br>modified, painted, produced, surface coated, or conjured partially or<br>completely into the final form in which it will be used on the ROBOT|
|**FIELD**|an approximately 144 in. by 144 in. (365.75 cm by 365.75 cm) area bounded<br>by the inside surface of the walls|
|**FIELD STAFF**|volunteers present in and around the ARENA that are responsible for making<br>sure the MATCHES are cycled through efficiently, fairly, safely, and with a<br>spirit of cooperation,_Gracious Professionalism_<sup>_®_</sup>, and generosity of spirit|
|**FLOWER**|a structure on the FIELD in which POLLEN and NECTAR can be placed into<br>the top, and POLLEN can be removed from the bottom|
|**FTA**|_FIRST_ Technical Advisor|
|**GARDEN**|an approximately 23 in. (58.40 cm) by 2 in. (5.10 cm) wide and infinitely tall<br>volume defined by the outside edge of blue or red tape in opposite corners of<br>the FIELD|
|**HIVE**|a bi-stable structure made up of two CELLS and a connecting assembly that<br>rotates on a pivot|
|**HOME REGION**|the region in which a team is assigned and in which they are only eligible to<br>advance from events within|
|**HUMAN PLAYER**|a SCORING ELEMENT manager|
|**INSPECTOR**|a person determined by _FIRST_ to accurately and efficiently assess the legality<br>of a given part or the whole of a ROBOT, an event volunteer role|
|**JUDGE**|volunteers who determine the teams that receive awards at events|
|**LAUNCH/LAUNCHING**|an action by a ROBOT in which the SCORING ELEMENT is shot into the air,<br>propelled across the floor to a desired location or in a preferred direction, or<br>thrown in a forceful way|
|**LEAVE**|a scoring achievement in which a ROBOT must move so that it is no longer<br>contacting the perimeter wall|
|**LOADING ZONE**|an approximately 23 in. (58.40 cm) wide by 11 in. (27.95 cm) deep infinitely<br>tall volume bounded by red or blue tape and the adjoining FIELD perimeters|
|**LRI**|Lead ROBOT INSPECTOR|
|**MAJOR FOUL**|a credit of 20 points towards the opponent’s MATCH point total|
|**MAJOR MECHANISM**|a group of COMPONENTS and/or MECHANISMS assembled together to<br>address at least 1 game challenge: ROBOT movement, SCORING ELEMENT<br>manipulation, FIELD element manipulation, or performance of a scorable task<br>without the assistance of another ROBOT|
|**MATCH**|a 30-second AUTO period, an 8-second transition period between AUTO and<br>TELEOP, and a 2-minute TELEOP period in which the ROBOT plays the current<br>season game|
|**MECHANISM**|an assembly of COMPONENTS that provide specific functionality on the<br>ROBOT. A MECHANISM can be disassembled (and then reassembled) into<br>individual COMPONENTS without damage to the parts.|
|**MINOR FOUL**|a credit of 5 points towards the opponent’s MATCH point total|
|**MOMENTARY**|describes durations that are fewer than approximately 3 seconds|
|**NECTAR**|approximately 3.6 in.(9.1 cm) Gopher ResisDent™ polyethylene balls|
|**OPERATOR CONSOLE**|the set of COMPONENTS and MECHANISMS used by the DRIVE TEAM to<br>relay commands to the ROBOT|
|**PARK**|a scoring achievement in which a ROBOT must move so that it is at least<br>partially in the LOADING ZONE|
|**PIN/PINNING**|an action by a ROBOT that is preventing the movement of an opponent<br>ROBOT by contact, either direct or transitive (such as against a FIELD<br>element)|
|**POLLEN**|2.8 in.(7.1 cm) Gopher ResisDent™ polyethylene balls in yellow|
|**POLLINATOR 1 RP**<br>**POLLINATOR 2 RP**|an RP earned when the number of TIPS at or above threshold<br>an RP earned when the number of TIPS at or above threshold|
|**PORTFOLIO**|a document used as part of the judging process with requirements outlined<br>in A201|
|**RANKING POINTS (RP)**|credited to a team based on their ALLIANCE’S performance in Qualification<br>MATCHES|
|**RANKING SCORE (RS)**|the average number of RANKING POINTS earned by a team throughout their<br>Qualification MATCHES|
|**RED CARD**|a penalty issued by the Head REFEREE for egregious ROBOT or team member<br>behavior or rule violations which results in a team being DISQUALIFIED for<br>the MATCH.|
|**REFEREE**|an official who is certified by _FIRST_ to enforce the rules of the current<br>season’s game, an event volunteer role|
|**REPEATED**|describes actions that happen more than once within a MATCH|
|**ROBOT**|an electromechanical assembly built by a _FIRST_ Tech Challenge team to play<br>the current season’s game and includes all the basic systems required to be<br>an active participant in the game –power, communications, control, and<br>movement about the FIELD|
|**ROBOT CONTROLLER**|Android device (a REV Control Hub (REV-31-1595) or a smartphone Android<br>device connected to a REV Expansion Hub (REV-31-1153)) that runs the<br>ROBOT CONTROLLER app to control the ROBOT as defined in R701|
|**ROBOT SIGN**|a required assembly which attaches to the ROBOT and simultaneously<br>identifies a ROBOT’S team number as well as its ALLIANCE affiliation|
|**SCORING ELEMENT**|SCORING ELEMENTS for BIOBUZZ are POLLEN and NECTAR|
|**STRATEGIC**|describes actions done with the aim of gaining a competitive advantage|
|**STARTING CONFIGURATION**|the physical configuration in which a ROBOT starts a MATCH|
|**STUDENT**|a person who has not completed high-school, secondary school, or the<br>comparable level in their HOME REGION as of September 1st|
|**SURROGATE**|a team randomly assigned by event management software to play an extra<br>Qualification MATCH|
|**SWARM RP**|an RP earned when the combined LEAVE + PARK points earned at or above<br>threshold|
|**TELEOP**|third period of each MATCH is 2 minutes (2:00) long and called the<br>teleoperated period (TELEOP). During TELEOP, DRIVERS remotely operate<br>ROBOTS|
|**TILE**|flooring surface of the FIELD is made of 36 interlocking soft foam TILES|
|**TIP/TIPPED**|a scoring criteria in which the HIVE moves from one stable state to the other<br>stable state with the downwards-facing CELL becoming the upwards-facing<br>CELL and the damper on the HIVE that was previously not contacting the<br>frame begins to contact the frame|
|**VENDOR**|a legitimate business source for COTS items that satisfies criteria defined in<br>Section 12 ROBOT Construction Rules (R)|
|**VERBAL WARNING**|a warning issued by event staff or the Head REFEREE|
|**YELLOW CARD**|a warning issued by the Head REFEREE for egregious ROBOT or team<br>member behavior or rule violations|
